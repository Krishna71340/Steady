# Steady

Steady is a simple and clean study + focus web app made to help students stay consistent.  
It focuses on building daily momentum instead of forcing extreme productivity.

The app works fully in the browser, stores everything locally, and does not need any login.

---

## What is Steady?

Steady helps you manage subjects, chapters, exams, and focus sessions in one place.  
It intelligently suggests what you should study next and tracks your progress over time.

The idea behind Steady is simple:  
small steps, done daily, lead to real progress.

---

## Features

Steady includes subject and chapter management where you can add subjects, split them into chapters, and mark chapters as completed. Each chapter can have a difficulty level which affects recommendations.

You can add exams with dates, and Steady automatically calculates urgency and prioritizes related chapters when exams are near.

The focus mode is inspired by the Pomodoro technique. You can start focused study sessions, track how long you studied, and view your focus history.

Steady also includes analytics like daily focus time, completed chapters, study streaks, and a heatmap view to visualize consistency.

All data is saved locally using browser storage, so Steady works offline and keeps your data private.

---

## Smart Recommendations

Steady uses a scoring system to recommend what you should study next.  
It considers:

- Chapter difficulty  
- Exam urgency  
- How often a chapter was skipped  
- How old the chapter is  
- Your selected study preset (light, balanced, exam, revision)

This helps you avoid decision fatigue and just start studying.

The goal is not to study harder —
